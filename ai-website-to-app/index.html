AI builders

Turn your AI-built website into a real app

AI builders make a working web app from a prompt, but they stop at the browser. Here is how to get the app you built onto phones and into Google Play, without learning Android development.

Convert my AI app Get the AppMint app

Short answer. Publish your app from the AI builder so it has a public address, then paste that address into the converter. You get a signed Android app (APK) and the Play Store file (AAB). Every change you publish in the builder appears in the app.

Things to check in an AI-built app

  • Sign-in: test Google sign-in and email links inside the app before you publish.
  • API keys: keep secret keys on your server, never in the browser code. Anyone who installs the app can read browser code.
  • Phone screens: ask the AI builder to make every page mobile-friendly first.
  • Camera, microphone and location: switch on the permissions your app uses in the builder's settings step.

Why put an AI app on Google Play?

A store listing gives your project a real home: people find it by searching Google Play, install it with one tap, and get your notifications. It is the fastest way to turn a weekend AI project into something people keep on their phone.

Questions

Do I need to export the code from the AI builder?

No. If the app is published at a public address, the converter only needs that address.

Will changes I make in the AI builder show up in the app?

Yes. Publish them in the builder and the app shows the new version, with no rebuild.

Is it free?

Yes. Converting and downloading the app is free, with no watermark.
