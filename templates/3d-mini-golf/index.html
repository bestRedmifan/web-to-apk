3D Mini Golf

Drag back to putt across six 3D mini golf holes with corners, blocks and a sliding bar, and finish the round in as few strokes as you can.

Play full screen Get this template in AppMint

Like it? Make your own Android game like 3D Mini Golf: AppMint is free on Google Play, open Templates and pick it.

How to play

Sink the ball in six holes with corners, blocks and a sliding bar. The fewest strokes wins.

On a phone or tablet

  • Drag back from anywhere to aim with the arrow
  • The farther you pull, the harder the putt
  • Let go to putt

With a mouse

  • Click and drag back to aim with the arrow
  • Pull farther for a harder putt, release to putt
