Tic-Tac-Toe

Play a friend or the computer at three difficulty levels, with undo and a running score.

Play full screen Get this template in AppMint

Like it? Make your own Android game like Tic-Tac-Toe: AppMint is free on Google Play, open Templates and pick it.

How to play

Get three of your marks in a row before your opponent does. Play the computer or a friend on the same screen.

On a phone or tablet

  • Tap a square to place your mark
  • Pick vs AI or 2 Players at the top
  • Choose Easy, Medium or Unbeatable
  • Tap Undo to take back a move

With a mouse

  • Click a square to place your mark
  • Pick vs AI or 2 Players at the top
  • Click Undo to take back a move

  • Vibrate
  • Two players
  • Scores
