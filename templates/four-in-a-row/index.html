Drop Four

Drop discs into a seven-column board and connect four in a line, against a minimax computer opponent on three difficulty levels or a friend on the same phone.

Play full screen Get this template in AppMint

Like it? Make your own Android game like Drop Four: AppMint is free on Google Play, open Templates and pick it.

How to play

Drop discs into the board and connect four in a row, across, up or diagonally, before your opponent.

On a phone or tablet

  • Touch a column: see where the disc will land
  • Lift your finger: drop the disc

With a mouse

  • Move over a column: see where the disc lands
  • Click: drop the disc
