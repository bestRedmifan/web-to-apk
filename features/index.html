Questions

Is FreeWebToApk really free?

Yes. Building the APK and the Play Store AAB is free and unlimited, with no signup and no watermark. The website is supported by ads on our own pages.

Can I publish the app on Google Play?

Yes. Every build gives you an AAB, the format Google Play requires, signed and ready to upload. Keep the same package name for every update.

How do I update an app I already published?

Build again with the same package name and a higher version (for example 1.0.1). The same package always gets the same signing key, so the new build installs as an update. For changes without a release, use Remote Update.

Can I add ads and earn money?

Yes. Add your AdMob App ID and ad unit IDs: banner, interstitial, rewarded, rewarded interstitial, medium rectangle and app-open ads are all supported, automatically placed or controlled by your page.

Does it support push notifications?

Yes, through your own Firebase project (upload google-services.json) or OneSignal.

Can I turn HTML, a ZIP or an AI-built site into an app?

Yes. Paste HTML, upload an HTML file or a ZIP of a built site (Lovable, Bolt, v0, Google AI Studio, Vite, React builds), and it ships inside the app and works offline.

Which permissions will my app have?

Only the ones you tick in App Settings. Your app declares exactly those, so Google Play and Play Protect see nothing extra.

Is my app safe to share?

Yes. Apps are built and signed on our server; the builder and signing keys are never exposed in the browser, and your uploaded files are not kept.
