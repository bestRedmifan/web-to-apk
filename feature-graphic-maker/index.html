Feature graphic maker (1024 x 500)

Google Play will not publish a listing without a feature graphic: a 1024 x 500 banner at the top of your store page. Type your app name and one line about it, pick your colours, and download it.

    How to use it

    1. Type the app name exactly as it appears in your listing.
    2. Add one line that says what the app does for the reader.
    3. Use your logo's colours; the palette extractor can pull them from your logo.
    4. Download and upload it in Play Console under Main store listing, Feature graphic.

    What works on a feature graphic

    It is shown small, and often partly covered by a play button when you add a promo video. Keep text large and near the centre, use at most one line besides the name, and avoid putting "Download now" or ratings on it: Google treats those as misleading metadata.

    Questions

    What format is the feature graphic?

    1024 x 500 pixels, JPEG or 24-bit PNG without transparency.

    Is the feature graphic required?

    Yes, for every app on Google Play.

    Can I reuse my website banner?

    Only if it reads well at small size. Website heroes usually carry too much text; a simpler version works better.
