Questions

Does Google Play need a privacy policy URL?

Yes. Every app on Google Play must link a privacy policy in Play Console, and it must be a public web address, not a PDF. Apps that use the camera, location, contacts, ads or accounts are checked most closely.

Where is my policy stored?

Nowhere. The whole policy is packed into the link itself, so the page you get works as long as freewebtoapk.com does, and we keep no copy of it. Changing a detail gives you a new link.

Is this legal advice?

No. It is a clear starting policy that matches what an app built here does. If your app collects more (accounts, payments, health data), add those sections or ask a lawyer.
