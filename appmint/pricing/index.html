Pricing FAQ

Is everything really free?

Yes. Every feature is free for everyone: unlimited APK and AAB builds, no watermark, AdMob ads, in-app selling, push notifications, Remote Update, custom signing keys and native plugins. AppMint is supported by a few ads inside the AppMint app itself. The apps you build carry no ads from us.

Is Remove Ads a subscription?

No. Remove Ads is a one-time purchase of $2 through Google Play that takes the ads out of the AppMint app for good. It stays on your Google account, so it survives a reinstall. It adds no features to your apps, because those are already free.

What does Support Development include?

For $10 a year through Google Play you get no ads inside the AppMint app, custom bug fixing for the apps you build, your feature requests taken up, a priority support channel, and you keep AppMint development going. Cancel anytime in Google Play; the ads simply come back when it lapses.

I bought Pro or ProMax earlier. What changes for me?

Nothing. Pro is the same product as Remove Ads and ProMax is the same product as Support Development, under their old names. You keep exactly what you paid for, and your apps keep working as before.

Does AppMint work offline?

No. AppMint needs an internet connection to build and sign apps. The apps you build can work offline if their content is bundled.

Can I publish a Free app on Google Play?

Yes. Google Play needs an Android App Bundle (AAB) for new apps, and every AppMint build gives you one, signed and ready to upload.

Do I need credits to use AI App Studio?

No. You can use your own key from Groq or Cloudflare, both of which have free tiers, or a paid provider of your choice. Credits are only for building with AppMint AI, where you don’t need a key.

Does AppMint take a cut of my ad or purchase revenue?

No. AdMob pays you directly, and in-app purchases go through your own Google Play account with 0% commission to AppMint.

Where do I buy Remove Ads, Support Development or credits?

Inside the AppMint app, on the Pricing screen. Install it from Google Play first.
