How to turn a website into an app

  1. Enter your website address

    Any public website works: WordPress, Shopify, Wix, Squarespace, Webflow, a site you coded yourself, or an app you made with an AI builder such as Lovable, Bolt or v0.

  2. Give it a name and an icon

    Upload your logo or let the builder make an icon from your initials. Pick a launch screen colour and whether the app opens in portrait, landscape or both.

  3. Choose the extras you want

    Push notifications, pull to refresh, a bottom tab bar, a side menu, offline error page, file uploads and downloads, and AdMob ads if you want to earn from the app.

  4. Build and download

    The converter signs the app and gives you two files: an APK to install on any Android phone right away, and an AAB to upload to Google Play.

What you get

  • A signed Android app, with your name, icon and package name.
  • Both files every time: APK for direct installs, AAB for the Play Store.
  • The same signing key for the same package name, so you can ship updates later.
  • No watermark and no build limit. Make as many apps and updates as you need.
  • No coding. Everything is set with switches and boxes.

Will my website work as an app?

If it works in Chrome on a phone, it works in the app: the app shows your live site, so every change you publish appears in the app without a new build. Logins, carts, forms and payments keep working the way they do on your site. For a site that should also open with no internet connection, upload a ZIP of the site instead and everything is packed inside the app.

Questions

Is the website to app converter really free?

Yes. Building, downloading and updating apps is free, with no build limit and no watermark.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. You type your website address, choose options with switches, and download the finished app.

Can I put the app on Google Play?

Yes. Every build includes the AAB file Google Play asks for. You publish it from your own Google Play developer account.

Does my app update when I change my website?

Yes. The app shows your live website, so content changes appear in the app right away. You only build again to change the app itself, such as its name, icon or settings.
