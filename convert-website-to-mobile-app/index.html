How to convert your website into a mobile app

Your customers already use your website on their phones. A mobile app puts it one tap away on their home screen, sends them notifications and lists your business on Google Play. Here is how to do it without hiring a developer.

Start converting Get the AppMint app

Short answer. Make your site work well on a phone, then use a website to app converter to wrap it in an Android app. Add your logo, turn on push notifications, download the AAB file and publish it on Google Play from your own developer account.

Before you start: is your site mobile-ready?

  • Open your site on your phone. Text should be readable without zooming, and buttons easy to tap.
  • Check that sign-in, checkout and contact forms work on the phone.
  • Make sure the site loads over https://.

Most sites built with WordPress, Shopify, Wix, Squarespace or Webflow pass these checks already.

Step by step

  1. Open the converter

    Go to the app builder and enter your website address and app name.

  2. Brand it

    Upload your logo as the app icon and choose your brand colour for the launch screen and the top bar.

  3. Add app features

    Turn on push notifications to bring customers back, a bottom tab bar for your main pages, and an offline page for when the connection drops.

  4. Test it on your phone

    Install the APK on an Android phone and go through your most important pages, such as products, booking and checkout.

  5. Publish on Google Play

    Create a Google Play developer account, upload the AAB, add screenshots and a privacy policy, and submit. Our Play Store checklist lists every step.

Questions

How long does it take to convert a website to an app?

Building the app takes a few minutes. Google Play reviews a new app before it goes live, which usually takes from a few hours to several days.

Will I need to rebuild the app when my website changes?

No. The app loads your live website, so new products, posts and prices appear in the app automatically.

Can I make an iPhone app this way?

No, this converter makes Android apps. iPhone users can add your site to their home screen as a web app; our free PWA builder helps you set that up.
