How to use it

  1. Upload the largest version of your logo or favicon you have (1024 x 1024 is ideal).
  2. Pick a background colour so a wide or transparent logo sits on a solid square.
  3. Check the round and squircle previews: nothing important should touch the edges.
  4. Download the 512 x 512 PNG for Play Console, and use the same image in step 2 of the app builder.

From a website logo to an app icon

A text logo that looks fine in a website header is unreadable at launcher size. Use just the symbol or the first letter of your brand, large and centred. If you have neither, the builder's icon step can make a clean letter icon for you.

Why the app looks different from the Play listing

The Play listing uses the 512 x 512 image you upload in Play Console. The phone's home screen uses the icon inside the app, which the builder creates from the image you give it, as an adaptive icon. Give both the same artwork so the two match.

Questions

Does Google Play accept a JPEG icon?

No. The store icon must be a 512 x 512 PNG, up to 1 MB.

Should I round the corners?

No. Upload a square. Google Play and each phone apply their own shape.

Why is my icon tiny with a white border on the phone?

The icon was drawn too small for the adaptive icon's safe area, or it was not adaptive at all. Apps built here always get a proper adaptive icon; give the builder a square image with the logo filling most of it.
