HTML to App: your HTML, CSS and JavaScript as an Android app

Made a game, a calculator, a portfolio or a tool with HTML? Turn it into an Android app that opens straight from the phone, with no server and no internet connection needed.

Upload my HTML Get the AppMint app

Short answer. Upload one HTML file, or a ZIP of your project with index.html at the top. The builder packs every file inside the app, signs it, and gives you an APK and a Play Store AAB. The app works offline.

What works inside the app

  • Your pages, styles, scripts, images, fonts and sounds, all from the phone.
  • Links between your pages, localStorage and IndexedDB for saving progress.
  • Calls to online APIs when the phone is connected.
  • Full screen and landscape for games.

Tips for a smooth app

  • Use relative paths such as images/logo.png, not paths on your computer like C:\.
  • Add <meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1"> so it fits phone screens.
  • Bundle fonts and libraries in the ZIP so the app looks right offline.

Questions

Does the HTML app need internet?

No. Everything in your file or ZIP is packed into the app and opens from the phone. Only calls you make to online services need a connection.

Do I need Android Studio?

No. The app is built online and signed for you.

Can I publish an HTML app on Google Play?

Yes. Every build includes the AAB file for Google Play.
