Questions people ask

Is the AppMint AI app builder really free?

Yes. Making apps is 100% free: no price, no watermark, no limits and no account needed. Use AppMint AI, or your own key from the AI company you choose and pay them directly at their price. Making apps never costs anything here.

Can I make an Android app with AI without coding?

Yes. Describe your app in your own words — or drop a screenshot, a sketch or a screen recording — and the AI writes the app, runs it live, fixes its own mistakes and gives you a real Android app (APK) you can install.

Can I put the app on Google Play?

Yes. You get a signed AAB file for Google Play and a signed APK to install, both with your app's own key, so later updates install over the old version.

Can my app have sign-in, a database and payments?

Yes. Connect your own free Supabase account and the AI adds sign-in, online saving that syncs across phones, and payments, with each person seeing only their own data.

Is it a good Lovable, Bolt or Replit alternative?

Yes, especially for phone apps. Those tools charge a monthly plan and keep your project on their servers. Here you can use your own AI key and pay your AI company directly, your code stays on your computer, and you get a real Android app, not just a website.

Do I need to install anything?

No. AppMint AI Studio runs in your web browser on a computer or phone. If you like, you can also use the AppMint Android app to make apps right on your phone.
