Tools to launch your app

Building the app is step one. Google Play also needs an icon, screenshots, a feature graphic, descriptions and a privacy policy, and your customers need to hear about it. Each tool below runs in your browser and is free, with no signup.

📱

Screenshot mockup generator

Put screenshots of your new app into phone frames with a headline and background, then export Play Store sized images.

 🎨

App icon resizer

Turn one logo into the 512x512 Google Play icon and every Android launcher size, and preview it under round and squircle masks.

 🖼️

Feature graphic maker (1024 x 500)

Make the 1024 x 500 feature graphic every Google Play listing needs, with your app name, a short tagline and your brand colours.

 👁️

Google Play listing preview

Preview how your app's Google Play page will look with your icon, title, short description and screenshots, before you submit it for review.

 📲

QR code generator for your app

Make a QR code that opens your app on Google Play, or downloads your APK, for posters, shop counters, flyers and business cards.

 📝

App description templates

Fill-in templates for your Google Play short description (80 characters) and full description (4000), written for apps made from websites.

 🎨

Color palette extractor

Upload your logo and get its main colours as hex codes, ready for your app's splash screen, app bar, icon background and store graphics.

 🎬

App promo video and GIF maker

Turn your app screenshots into a short promo video or animated GIF with text and transitions, for YouTube, social posts and your website.

 📣

Social media kit for your app launch

Make matching launch images for Instagram, Facebook, X, LinkedIn and WhatsApp Status from your app icon and one headline.

 ☑️

Play listing checklist

Everything Google Play asks for before your first release, ticked off one by one.

 📜

Privacy policy link

A privacy policy with a public link for Play Console, made in a minute.

 🔄

Remote update file

Change an installed app without a new release: start page, announcements, maintenance.

 ⚡

PWA builder

Make your website installable from the browser, with a manifest and offline page.

In what order?

  1. Build the app and install it on your phone.
  2. Make the 512 x 512 icon and pick your colours with the palette extractor.
  3. Capture screenshots in the app and frame them with the mockup generator.
  4. Make the feature graphic, write the text with the description templates, and check it all in the listing preview.
  5. Get a privacy policy link, then work through the listing checklist in Play Console.
  6. After launch: a QR code for your shop, a promo video and a social media kit.
