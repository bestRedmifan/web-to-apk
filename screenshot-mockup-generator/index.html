How to use it

  1. Take screenshots inside the app you built (not the website in a browser): open it on your phone and press power + volume down.
  2. Upload them, choose a frame and a background colour taken from your logo.
  3. Write a headline of five words or fewer for each screen, the benefit rather than the feature name.
  4. Download, then upload them in Play Console under Main store listing, Phone screenshots.

Which screens to show

Show what someone does in the first minute: the home screen, the one thing your app is for (ordering, booking, reading, playing), and one screen that proves it is a real app, such as the side menu, a notification or the offline screen. Skip the login page and the cookie banner.

Two to eight screenshots are allowed. Four good ones beat eight similar ones.

Getting clean screenshots from a website app

Close pop-ups and chat bubbles before you capture. If your site shows a "download our app" banner, hide it inside the app with a line of custom CSS so it never appears in the app or in your screenshots.

Questions

What size do Play Store phone screenshots need to be?

Google Play takes PNG or JPEG images between 320 and 3840 pixels on each side, with the long side no more than twice the short side. The tool exports within those limits.

Can I use screenshots of my website in a desktop browser?

You can, but reviewers and users expect to see the app on a phone. Capture the installed app instead; it is also what Play reviewers compare against.

Are my screenshots uploaded anywhere?

No. The mockups are drawn in your browser and never leave your computer.
