Content Rating & Declarations

⏱️ 10-15 minutes

🔞 Content Rating Questionnaire

💡
For WebView Apps: If your website has user-generated content, ads, or links to external sites, mention this in the questionnaire.

📋 App Declarations

🔐 Common App Permissions

🌐
INTERNET Required for WebView apps
📁
WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE For file downloads
📷
CAMERA For photo/video uploads
🔔
POST_NOTIFICATIONS For push notifications
⚠️
Important: Only request permissions your app actually uses. Google may reject apps with unnecessary permissions.
6

Closed Testing

⏱️ 14 days minimum

⚠️ REQUIRED for New Developer Accounts

🧪 Closed Testing Requirements

Google requires new developers to run a closed test before publishing to production.

12+ Testers who opt-in
14 Days of continuous testing
20+ Countries (testers from)
🎯
Where to Find Testers:
  • Friends & family with Android phones
  • Social media followers
  • Reddit communities (r/androidapps, r/betatester)
  • Facebook groups for beta testers
  • Your website visitors
